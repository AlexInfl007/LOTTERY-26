Seren Lottery Chain

Seren Lottery Chain is a transparent on-chain lottery on Polygon. The project highlights verifiable randomness, a public smart contract, and an open jackpot flow that users can inspect on Polygonscan.

The application lets visitors connect a wallet, track the current prize pool, review recent winners, and understand how Chainlink VRF helps make winner selection fair and auditable.
